## Суть урока

Обзор публикации в store: **Google Play** ($25 one-time) vs **Apple Developer** ($99/year for App Store publish; free tier can build/test). Listing assets: icon, screenshots, description, content rating, privacy policy. **Internal/closed testing** tracks перед production. IAP products должны существовать в **Play Console / App Store Connect**, совпадая с Unity catalog (15.4). Review times и rejection reasons (crashes, metadata, IAP). Version codes/build numbers monotonic. Связать analytics + crash monitoring post-launch (15.3).
