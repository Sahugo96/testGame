## Суть урока

**Unity IAP** через **Services → In-App Purchasing**: org + project id, включает **Unity Analytics** revenue tracking. **IAP Catalog** (`Window → Unity IAP`): products — **Consumable** / **Non-Consumable** / **Subscription**; locales, Google price vs Apple tiers; store export (Apple screenshot required). UI: **IAP Button** + **Codeless IAP** callbacks (success/fail); **Restore purchases**; optional auto-fill title/price from store. Регистрация продуктов на стороне store обязательна (Google Play + App Store). Demo — не production receipt validation server.
